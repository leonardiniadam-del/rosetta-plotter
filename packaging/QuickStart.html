Rosetta Plotter Quick Start

Plot your drawing on an AxiDraw in a few steps.

1. Install

  1. Double-click RosettaPlotterSetup.
  2. Windows may show a blue box that says “Windows protected your PC”. This is normal for this app. Click More info, then click Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. It installs just for you, so you don't need an administrator password.
  4. If your laptop is missing a part called Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, the installer adds it for you. If it says it needs administrator rights for that, ask your instructor or IT help desk.
  5. Open Rosetta Plotter from the Start menu (or the desktop shortcut, if you made one).

2. Connect the plotter

  1. Plug the AxiDraw's power adapter into the wall and into the plotter.
  2. Plug the USB cable into the plotter and into your laptop.
  3. The status bar in the app shows the plotter's name when it's connected. If it says Model unknown, choose Plotter › Plotter model… and pick your AxiDraw.
The power adapter matters: without it, the pen can't move even though the app sees the plotter.

3. Set Home

Home is the HOME corner (next to the HOME label and the USB port). The app measures everything from there.

  1. Click Set Home (in the Plotter menu or the tool strip). The motors let go.
  2. Gently slide the pen carriage by hand into the HOME corner, as far as it goes.
  3. Click Set Home again. Press Esc instead if you want to cancel.
Do this every time you turn the plotter on or move the carriage by hand.

4. Import your drawing

  1. Click Import in the Jobs list (or File › Import…, Ctrl+I), or drag a file onto the window. SVG, PDF and Illustrator (.ai) files work, including the .ai files Rhino exports.
  2. For a file with several pages, pick the page you want.
  3. A ⚠ next to a job means something needs a look. Hover over it to read why.
You can also print into Rosetta Plotter. Once, from the Start menu, run Install the Rosetta Plotter printer (it asks for administrator permission). After that, with Rosetta Plotter open, choose Print in any program and pick the Rosetta Plotter printer: the page shows up in the Jobs list a few seconds later. Print lines as vectors, at 100%.

5. Place it on the paper

  1. Choose your Paper size in the side panel.
  2. Double-click the job to place it in the paper's corner nearest Home, or drag it onto the paper.
  3. Drag it to move it. Drag a corner handle to scale (hold Shift to keep its proportions). Drawings come in at 100%, so scale drawings stay to scale.
  4. As you drag, the drawing snaps to the paper's edges, margins and center. Hold Alt to drag freely. Object › Snap to paper edge puts it flush against an edge.
  5. Anything shown in red is off the paper and won't be drawn.
  6. Tape your paper down on the plotter so it lines up with the paper on screen.

6. Plot

  1. Optional: Trace outline moves the pen (raised) around your drawing so you can check where it lands.
  2. Put a pen in the holder and click Start (Ctrl+Enter).
  3. Pause stops the pen safely. Resume carries on. Return Home sends the pen to the HOME corner. Stop ends the plot for good.
In the Plotter panel, the arrows move the pen by the step you pick (0.1 to 100 mm), the button in the middle sends it Home, Pen up and Pen down raise and lower it, and Release motors lets you slide the pen by hand (use Set Home afterwards).

7. Pen changes

  1. If your drawing has more than one color, the pen goes back Home and the app asks for the next pen, for example “Put the red pen in the holder (2 of 3), then click Continue.” The message is in the bar above the paper.
  2. Swap the pen, then click Continue.
  3. Optional, when the new pen must land exactly on the last pen's lines: while the app waits for the pen, the Plotter panel still works. Click a drawn line with the Move pen tool, click Pen down, and nudge with the arrows (0.1, 0.5 or 1 mm) until the nib sits on the line. The panel shows how far you nudged; when you click Continue, this pen is plotted that far over.

If something goes wrong

  • Plotter disconnected: check the USB cable and the power adapter, plug it back in, then click Continue.
  • The app asks you to slide the pen Home: slide it into the HOME corner (next to the HOME label and the USB port), then click It's in the corner.
  • Still stuck? Choose File › Copy diagnostics and send that text to your instructor.
